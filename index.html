02 why cpa, not roas

I optimize for cost per acquisition. ROAS is the side effect.

ROAS is a ratio. It looks great when you sell to people who were going to buy anyway, and it collapses the moment you try to grow. CPA is a cost. It tells you exactly what a new customer costs today, and whether you can afford more of them tomorrow.

01 · roas rewards shrinking

The easiest way to raise ROAS is to spend less

Cut prospecting, retarget harder, and the ratio goes up while the business stays flat. A CPA target forces the only question that matters: how many customers can I buy this month at a price that still makes money?

02 · cpa connects to the p&l

Margin minus CPA is profit per order

That is a number a founder can act on: raise budget, cut it, or fix the offer. A 4x ROAS says nothing about whether the 4 covers product cost, shipping and returns. A $26 CPA against a known margin does.

03 · cpa is how meta scales

Cost caps and bid caps speak CPA

Meta's bidding runs on cost per result, not on ROAS. When the account is built around a CPA target, the algorithm, the budget and the founder are all reading the same number, and spend can grow without the structure breaking. Astra Straps runs on $13–15 cost caps. Urban Air holds $48.33 across $5.5M.

04 · roas still gets reported

I track it. I just don't chase it.

Blended ROAS, MER and net profit live in TripleWhale on every account, and they are on this page. But the target I set, test against and scale on is CPA by product and funnel stage. Hit that, and ROAS and profit take care of themselves.

target_cpa.jshow I set the target
// what a customer can cost before the order loses money
const breakEvenCPA = aov * grossMargin;

// what a customer should cost to hit the profit you want per order
const targetCPA    = breakEvenCPA - desiredProfitPerOrder;

if (actualCPA <= targetCPA)  scale();          // raise budget, ship more ads
else                         fixOffer();       // creative, funnel stage or pricing. not "wait for roas"

Smoovee
kitchen · e-commerce · USA

Brand-new product launched cold in the US. Lookalikes, UGC creatives, and bid caps on Meta. 42 campaigns tested, winners scaled. The product sold out.

$33.76 best CPP117 purchases · 1 ad setsold out
+full story
challenge

Launching a brand-new product in the US with no audience and no brand recognition. Find winning audiences and creatives fast, control CPP, and build a full-funnel structure from scratch.

strategy

Parallel Meta campaigns with Lookalike Audiences, UGC-style creatives, and bid cap strategies to control CPP. 42 campaigns ran through systematic testing: losers killed fast, winners re-tested and scaled. Mother's Day used as a seasonal scaling angle.

result

Best Meta ad set: 117 purchases at $49.70 CPP, with the best CPP at $33.76 on a bid cap CBO. Products drove to sold-out status, with live orders tracked in TripleWhale.

Eric Rich
high-ticket coaching · USA

$8K program sold to cold US traffic through the "Income Seeker" VSL series: free webinar, retargeting, call booking. Stable funnel at $2,000/day.

547 calls booked$4.87 best CPL1,003 webinar signups
+full story
challenge

An $8K high-ticket real estate and surplus funds program in the US market. Selling $8K directly to cold traffic burned budget with no return.

diagnosis

Without an educational VSL at the top of the funnel, leads arrived too cold for a call. Cost per booking was unsustainable and close rates were low.

strategy

Built the "Income Seeker" VSL series (Surplus Funds, Unclaimed Money, Government Glitch) as top-of-funnel entry. Best VSL drove signups at $4.87. Free webinar → retargeting → call booking → $8K offer.

result

$34K invested in call booking campaigns in April generated 547 booked calls and 1,003 webinar signups: a stable, scalable funnel at $2,000/day.

Pick My Brain
ai saas · silicon valley

Early-stage SaaS with zero audience. Awareness first, then aggressive retargeting of engaged users. CAC dropped as the funnel warmed up.

0 → 500 subscribers2,175 signups · 6 months10.86K MAU
+full story
challenge

Pick My Brain launched with no user base, entering an extremely competitive AI platform market in the US.

diagnosis

In early-stage SaaS, converting cold traffic directly to subscriptions rarely works. Awareness and authority had to come first.

strategy

Two-step funnel: awareness campaigns with valuable content, followed by aggressive retargeting of engaged users. CAC dropped as the funnel warmed up.

result

From zero to 500 paid subscriptions with consistent month-over-month growth across 3 international markets, with the USA as the primary market.

MOSSY
fashion · e-commerce · Brazil

Validated product stuck at R$30K/month: cost per order rose every time budget grew. Full funnel rebuild with stage-specific creatives unlocked scale.

R$30K → R$320K/mo10x ROAS held at scale
+full story
context

MOSSY had a validated product, but ROAS dropped every time they increased budget. The campaign structure could not scale.

diagnosis

The account did not separate cold traffic from retargeting. The algorithm mixed audiences and cost per purchase kept rising.

strategy

Full rebuild with top, mid, and bottom of funnel. Stage-specific creatives allowed scaling without hurting efficiency.

result

Consistent 10x ROAS with a 10x larger budget. The brand gained revenue predictability and real scaling capacity: R$30K to R$320K/month.

Casa Tropical
premium fashion · lifestyle

Premium brand with engagement but no sales. Growth campaigns (including a Saint Laurent collab) separated from conversion campaigns.

14.35x ROAS7K → 32K followersYSL collab
+full story
brand

Premium fashion with aspirational positioning. The challenge: grow qualified followers and convert that growth into sales.

diagnosis

Beautiful content, but campaigns generated engagement instead of sales. The funnel was incomplete.

strategy

Growth campaigns (including the Saint Laurent collab) fully separated from conversion campaigns (catalog, direct offer), each with its own structure.

result

14.35x ROAS, from 7K to 32K followers at R$0.81 average CPC. The Saint Laurent collab amplified reach and opened doors with luxury brands.

Bloom → Loum
fashion · rebranding · BR/PT

Complete rebrand launched without losing the existing base: retention retargeting in parallel with acquisition for the new brand.

19x ROASR$686K in conversions
+full story
rebrand

Bloom became Loum: complete rebrand, new identity, new name. The challenge was launching without losing the existing base while scaling sales.

risk

Double exposure: losing existing customers during the transition and not building a new audience fast enough.

strategy

Retargeting for the existing base (communicating the transition) running in parallel with acquisition for the new brand. Budget split strategically.

result

19x ROAS, the best in the portfolio, with R$686,000 in conversions recorded by Meta Ads and the new brand established in Brazil and Portugal.

Traffic Raptors
nutraceuticals · US/CA/EU/AU

Four countries running simultaneously, each with its own structure, creatives, and copy adapted to the local market.

3.4x to 5.3x ROAS4 markets at once
+full story
challenge

Running nutraceutical campaigns in the US, Canada, Europe, and Australia simultaneously, each market with its own rules and distinct audiences.

diagnosis

Generic creatives ignored cultural differences. What worked in one market did not convert in another.

strategy

Separate structure per market, with creatives and copy adapted for each country and systematic testing to find the specific conversion angles.

result

Consistent ROAS between 3.4x and 5.3x across all 4 countries. Sustainable scale because each market had its own optimized structure.
